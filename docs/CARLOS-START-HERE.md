## Questions to ask as a first-time user
- Is the displayed price a guide share or my full cost? Is it estimated or locked?
- What happens if a friend joins or leaves?
- Which QR is safe to share publicly?
- What do I still need to do before my personal pass unlocks?
- Who scans the pass, and what happens on a second scan?
