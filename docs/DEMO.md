# Akyat demo walkthrough

## Before recording
- Open one browser and keep the full journey in that browser. Bookings are local.
- Use Demo controls → Reset demo to restore the initial sample group.
- Keep browser zoom at 100%. Desktop and mobile layouts are available.
- Use fictional names/contact information, such as Alex Rivera / Jamie Demo / +63 900 000 0000.
- Explain once that payments and external participants are simulated.

## Core sequence
1. **Home:** “Find your people. Find your peak.” Point out the shared guide-fee example.
2. **Amihan Ridge:** Show difficulty, distance, ascent, duration, organizer, group members, and price breakdown.
3. **Join:** Three existing hikers plus the presenter make four. Guide share ₱450 plus ₱150 individual fees = ₱600 estimated total.
4. **Invite:** Show the public party QR and link. It can be posted on socials. In this prototype, another device opens its own seeded party, not synchronized participants.
5. **Price drop:** In Demo controls, add two friends. Six hikers share the same ₱1,800 guide fee: ₱300 each. Total becomes ₱450 including individual fees. Close the controls so the actual price panel is visible.
6. **Register:** Fill fictional name and emergency contact. Acknowledge the sample packing and participation steps; save.
7. **Lock:** Demo controls → Close registration. Explain that the final quote is now fixed and payment opens.
8. **Pay:** Continue to demo payment, review the policy, and simulate the ₱450 payment. No payment details are collected.
9. **Pass:** Show the personal QR and booking reference. This QR is private, unlike the invitation.
10. **Organizer:** Preview organizer check-in → Load my demo booking. Confirm that payment and requirements are complete, then Confirm check-in. The result becomes Already checked in.

## Optional short branches
- **Readiness:** Home → Find my pace. First hike + easy walk + forest suggests Luntian Forest Loop, with an explanation. This is not fitness clearance.
- **Minimum not reached:** Reset, select Luntian Forest Loop in Demo controls, close registration before joining. Two hikers are below its minimum of three; the departure cancels without payment.
- **Nonpayment:** After the presenter pays, end the payment window. Other reserved participants expire. The presenter's locked quote does not change; the organizer contribution is shown.
- **Cancellation:** A hiker cancellation at least 24 hours before departure produces a simulated refund. Late requests remain pending organizer review. Organizer cancellation refunds all paid bookings.
- **Incorrect QR:** Paste a party invitation URL into organizer lookup. It is identified as an invitation rather than an admission pass.

## Reliable recording paths
Use **Load my demo booking** or paste the booking reference if camera/file-picker support is unavailable. QR image generation and decoding are real, but camera permission is device-dependent. No live camera permission was requested during automated QA.

## Claims to avoid
Do not claim research interviews, official trail acceptance, real identity verification, real payment processing, synchronized bookings, emergency alerts, or production organizer authentication. The price-drop mechanic is a design hypothesis; uniqueness against all competitors has not been established.
