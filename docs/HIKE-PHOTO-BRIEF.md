## Where to put the photos
Carlos may add files directly to **`public/images/hikes/`**; this is an explicit addition to his ownership. Use the exact names above. The app is being wired to these paths. A temporary, visibly labeled illustration remains until the corresponding photo is supplied.

Also create `collaboration/carlos/photo-sources.md` with one entry per photo: hike, source URL, photographer/owner, permission or license, downloaded filename, and why the location is confirmed. Prefer your own/team photographs, permission from the photographer, or licensed sources. Google Images is a discovery tool, not permission to reuse. Preserve originals separately if you crop.

## Image specifications
- At least 1800 px wide; JPEG preferred, ideally under 700 KB after export.
- Landscape composition, approximately 3:2. The same file is cropped responsively for cards and detail headers.
- Keep the mountain's defining features in the center 70%; mobile crops the sides.
- For Talamitam's hero: a wide image with breathing room around the summit. We apply the color treatment in CSS; supply a natural-color original.
- Optional second images: `mt-talamitam-detail.jpg`, `mt-batulao-detail.jpg`, `mt-ulap-detail.jpg`. These are optional assets for the pitch/video, not required app inputs.
- No baked-in headings, watermarks added by aggregators, unrelated snow-covered alpine ranges, or AI-generated scenes represented as actual mountains.

## Keep the pitch honest
Trail names and broad locations are real. The app's selected route distance, ascent, duration, fees, meeting points, and schedules are **sample itinerary data**, not verified operating facts. Do not use these to give real trip advice. Opening status, local rules, capacity, current fees, and conditions require direct confirmation from the relevant organizer/LGU before a real launch.

The older Akyat site is a visual reference only. Do not copy its application code or download its existing image files into this hackathon repository.
